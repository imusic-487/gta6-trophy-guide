Platinum roadmap

Four phases, in order. The only things that can truly be lost are in phase 1 — everything else can be cleaned up after the story.

  1. Phase 1 — Play the story, protect the missables

    Chapters 1–4 and the finale earn 13 story trophies on their own. Before you start: turn the spoiler shield on, and make a manual save at the start of every chapter — there is no mission replay and no second playthrough. Two trophies can be missed outright: Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at the Chapter 1 balcony spot — walk past it and it is gone) and Strangers & Freaks (one timed step, Race Against Yago, in Chapter 3).

  2. Phase 2 — Strangers & side strands

    Finish the eight stranger mission strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret and the rest) and the activities they open — fishing with Cal, hunting with Bret, the jewelry jobs that hand you the Snap Gun. This is also when to try every activity once for Master of None.

  3. Phase 3 — Money, property & wheels

    Bank vaults open after the end-of-Chapter-4 mission “That’s Where The Money Is” (5 vaults for Maximum Withdrawal). Buy all 8 garages — about $160,000 — through the property app that opens after Daily Driver in Chapter 2, register 10 vehicles in the Scooter Bros app (after Moses), and launder marked bills toward the $250,000 Dime on the Dollar total.

  4. Phase 4 — Clean-up grinds

    After the story, mop up the counters: 250 stunts (100% completion wants 300), 42 gold Shooting Range medals, 14 Top Fun drone races, 100 vehicles discovered, 100 WAiNK spots, 3 card sets, 50 challenges, and full exploration of Leonida. Check the in-game stats screen against this list, then A Long Road and the platinum pop together.

⚠ The two missables

Positive Mental Attitude — the first meditation spot is a balcony in Chapter 1. Miss it and the trophy is unobtainable in that playthrough.

Strangers & Freaks — the Chapter 3 step Race Against Yago is timed. Do it when it appears; there is no way to replay the chapter.
